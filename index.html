Destination 01 • Flagship Hub

Sète

The "Venice of Languedoc", nestled between the open sea and the saltwater Thau lagoon. Historic fishing port, water jousting, oyster ateliers, and tranquil Mediterranean immersion away from mass tourism.

✓ Mont Saint-Clair panoramic view & canalside cafés
✓ Thau lagoon boat tours & oyster farming discovery
✓ Direct TGV access from Paris (3h40)

Frequently Asked Questions

How does an enquiry work?

You tell us your group size, destination interest, and learning goals. Our team reads your brief and proposes tailored dates, lodgings, ateliers, and exact pricing.

Why are prices not listed on the website?

Because every stay is custom-built (cohort size, season, accommodation type, excursion choices). We provide fully transparent, all-inclusive quotations in our custom proposals.

How do we travel to Sète?

Sète has a direct TGV station (3h40 from Paris Gare de Lyon). The nearest airports are Montpellier-Méditerranée and Béziers Cap d'Agde. Our coordinator greets you upon arrival.

Do you offer internships or work placements?

No. Formaskills Travel specializes strictly in educational group mobility, accredited French learning, cultural discovery, and au pair homestays.
