Four Verified Hubs

Explore France
from the coast to the capital.

From the tranquil saltwater canals of Sète to the university boulevards of Montpellier, the artistic light of Nice, and the iconic monuments of Paris.

Destination 01 · Flagship Operational Hub

Sète

The "Venice of Languedoc", nestled between the open Mediterranean and the saltwater Thau lagoon. Historic fishing port, water jousting, oyster ateliers, and authentic French conversation away from mass tourism.

✓ Mont Saint-Clair panoramic view & canalside living
✓ Thau lagoon boat tours & oyster farming workshops
✓ Direct TGV access from Paris (3h40)
Destination 02 · Academic Capital

Montpellier

Europe's historic university capital with 1,000 years of academic life. Just 20 minutes from Sète, Montpellier combines marble pedestrian boulevards in the historic Écusson with a vibrant international student community.

✓ Historic pedestrian Écusson & student language cafes
✓ Jardin des Plantes & Europe's oldest medical faculty
✓ Musée Fabre fine arts ateliers & modern tramway
Destination 03 · Culture & Heritage

Paris

An exceptional concentration of world-class museums, heritage sites, Latin Quarter literary salons, and Seine riverbanks. Ideal for cohorts looking for profound cultural depth and iconic architecture.

✓ Sorbonne salons, Latin Quarter & literary heritage
✓ Louvre, Musée d’Orsay & twilight architectural walks
✓ Full metro transit pass & vetted student lodging
Destination 04 · French Riviera

Nice

Where Mediterranean azure waters meet the Maritime Alps. The world-famous Promenade des Anglais, Vieux Nice flower market, coastal sailing ecology, and modern Riviera art (Matisse & Chagall).

✓ Promenade des Anglais & turquoise Baie des Anges
✓ Cours Saleya culinary & flower market ateliers
✓ Matisse & Chagall museum workshops

Frequently Asked Questions

How does an enquiry work?

Tell us your cohort size, target dates, and learning goals. Our team evaluates accommodations and ateliers, then provides an all-inclusive proposal with transparent pricing.

Why are prices not listed publicly?

Every stay is built uniquely based on cohort size, season, housing choices (host family, residence, villa), and custom excursions.

How do we travel to Sète?

Sète has direct TGV high-speed trains from Paris Gare de Lyon (3h40). The nearest airports are Montpellier-Méditerranée and Béziers. Our coordinator greets you on arrival.

Do you provide work placements or internships?

No. Formaskills Travel strictly coordinates language courses, cultural immersion, educational group mobility, and au pair homestays.
