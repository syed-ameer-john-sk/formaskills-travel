## 2. Core Service Pillars & Architecture

1. **For Individuals (`/en/for-me/`)**: French courses, cultural discoveries, host family and au pair stays.
2. **For Organisations (`/en/for-organisations/`)**: Erasmus+ mobility reception, tailored group stays, job shadowing, pedagogical alignment, 24/7 coordination.
3. **Four Core Destinations**:
   - **Sète (`/en/destinations/sete/`)**: Flagship coastal base, Thau saltwater lagoon, canals, seafood ecology, Mediterranean maritime atmosphere.
   - **Montpellier (`/en/destinations/montpellier/`)**: Academic & historic capital, Écusson old town, student vibe, 20 min from Sète.
   - **Paris (`/en/destinations/paris/`)**: Cultural heritage, museums, Latin Quarter, iconic landmarks, Seine.
   - **Nice (`/en/destinations/nice/`)**: French Riviera, Promenade des Anglais, Vieux Nice, art & light (Matisse, Chagall).
4. **Learn French (`/en/learn-french/`)**: Small groups, 1-on-1, conversational practice, FLE pedagogy.
5. **Au Pair & Host Families (`/en/au-pair-and-host-families/`)**: Immersive family living, vetted French hosts.
6. **Cultural & Local Activities (`/en/tourism/`)**: Guided visits, boat tours, culinary workshops, local heritage.
