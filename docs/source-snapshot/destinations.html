    Mediterranean coast

    Sète

    A lively port city between the Mediterranean Sea and the Thau Lagoon, Sète offers a relaxed coastal setting, local traditions and easy access to cultural and outdoor activities. It is particularly well suited to groups looking for a Mediterranean experience on a human scale.

    • Canals and harbour atmosphere
    • Beaches and coastal activities
    • Local traditions and gastronomy
    • Compact and easy to explore

    Recommended period July - August

    Explore Sète

    Historic and dynamic

    Montpellier

    Montpellier combines a historic city centre with a young, lively and international atmosphere. Its pedestrian streets, cultural venues and proximity to the Mediterranean make it a versatile destination for educational stays and group programmes.

    • Historic city centre
    • Young and cultural atmosphere
    • Easy access to the Mediterranean coast
    • Wide range of activities and visits

    Recommended period July

    Explore Montpellier

    Culture and heritage

    Paris

    Paris offers an exceptional concentration of museums, heritage sites, cultural institutions and educational opportunities. It is an ideal destination for groups looking for a rich programme combining learning, culture and major landmarks.

    • World-famous museums and landmarks
    • Major cultural institutions
    • Extensive educational opportunities
    • Excellent public transport network

    Recommended period May and June

    Explore Paris

    French Riviera

    Nice

    Nice combines Mediterranean scenery, historic neighbourhoods and an active cultural life. With the sea, the old town and easy access to the French Riviera, it is especially suited to programmes combining culture, outdoor activities and discovery.

    • Seafront and Mediterranean setting
    • Historic old town
    • Cultural and outdoor activities
    • Easy access to the French Riviera

    Recommended period September

    Explore Nice
