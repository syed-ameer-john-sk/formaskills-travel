Venice of Languedoc

Sète · Thau Lagoon

Known as the "Venice of Languedoc", Sète is a living fishing port caught between the open sea and the Thau saltwater lagoon. Famous for literary heritage (Paul Valéry, Georges Brassens), vibrant summer water jousts, and calm, unhurried French language immersion away from mass tourism.
